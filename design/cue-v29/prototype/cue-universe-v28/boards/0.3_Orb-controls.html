MOTION
The orb follows your finger 1:1 and never lags. Ring fades in over 120 ms on touch, turns once every 4 s while held and fades out over 220 ms. Stepped values snap with a spring (response 0.28, damping 0.72). The trail brightens while held.
PRECISION & HAPTICS
Slide down while holding for ½ and ¼ speed. Double-tap the orb to return to the default. Tap the value to type a number. Selection haptic on each step and on the detent, soft impact at both ends.
ACCESSIBILITY
44 × 44 pt target. VoiceOver: adjustable, reads label, value and unit (“Speed, 140 words per minute”); swipe up or down for one step. Value always in text. Reduce Motion: no spin or glow pulse, the orb still moves. Labels follow Dynamic Type.
