# Title scores (title packets of the Ultra study only)

In a title packet each script shows its title under "Title:". Score every script on the eight criteria of `rubric.md` as usual, and also
score three things about its title, 1–5 each. The context's "Subject:" line is the title of the idea the creator picked on a card or in
Find inspiration; the app lists the script under the title shown here.

- **title_match**: would the creator recognise the idea they picked in this title?
  - 5 the same idea and promise (the words may differ);
  - 4 the same idea, with one part of its promise dropped or narrowed;
  - 3 the same topic, but another promise or angle;
  - 1 another idea.
- **title_quality**: as this video's title in the creator's list and on the teleprompter.
  - 5 specific, scannable at a glance, says what the video is, in the creator's language and register;
  - 4 good, but longer than it needs or a little flat;
  - 3 generic, clumsy, or so long it reads as a sentence;
  - 1 misleading or broken.
  - When the title shows "subtitle:", judge the pair as the app shows it (the idea's title with a line under it): the subtitle must add this
    script's own take without repeating the title's words.
- **delivers_title**: does the script deliver what its title (with the subtitle, when shown) promises?
  - 5 fully; 4 almost (one promised part thin); 3 partly; 1 not.

Write them as `"title_match"`, `"title_quality"` and `"delivers_title"` next to the eight criteria of each script in your JSON. Never let a
title score change the eight criteria, which judge the script alone.
